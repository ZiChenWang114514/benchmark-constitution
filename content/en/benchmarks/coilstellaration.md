# CoilStellaration

A stellarator coil-design benchmark of 266,549 samples over 14,496 plasma boundaries, asking models to generate a feasible coilset from a target boundary and engineering requirements in one pass. The paper reports the best baseline mean soft-feasibility score at only 0.13 and strict feasibility at 4.4%.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Given a target plasma boundary and a set of engineering requirements, can a model generate an engineerable coilset in a single forward pass that meets both magnetic-field accuracy and geometric constraints?

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12689) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12689) [3](https://github.com/proximafusion/coilstellaration) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/proxima-fusion/coilstellaration) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper describes the existing QUASR repository of nearly 370,000 quasi-axisymmetric and quasi-helically symmetric devices with coilsets; CoilStellaration targets conditional coilset generation and systematically explores trade-offs between engineering requirements and field accuracy across aspect ratios, rotational transforms and coil lengths.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12689) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12689) [3](https://github.com/proximafusion/coilstellaration) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/proxima-fusion/coilstellaration) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Construction

**Question sources**：For each plasma boundary and sampled requirement set, REGCOIL produces a warm-start coilset that DESC then refines; a full record of physics and engineering metrics is computed for every coilset.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12689) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12689) [3](https://github.com/proximafusion/coilstellaration) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/proxima-fusion/coilstellaration) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Average normalized field error (the community gold standard); normalized violations of four engineering constraints mapped by a saturating exponential to a 0 to 1 soft-feasibility score; strict feasibility rate

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12689) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12689) [3](https://github.com/proximafusion/coilstellaration) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/proxima-fusion/coilstellaration) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Scoring**：Generated coilsets are re-evaluated with the same DESC filamentary pipeline used for data generation; per-metric tolerances are frozen before evaluation and saturation prevents trading surplus on one metric against slack on another.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12689) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12689) [3](https://github.com/proximafusion/coilstellaration) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/proxima-fusion/coilstellaration) (accessed 2026-11-16)
