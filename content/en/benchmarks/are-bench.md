# ARE-bench

A research-agent benchmark of 75 tasks drawn from real researcher sessions with replayed workspaces, spanning seven research stages and scored by 1,173 binary checklist items. The paper reports the strongest model, GPT-6.1-Sol, passing every item on only 66.2% of tasks.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Whether research agents can advance one step of an ongoing project while respecting earlier session decisions and handling workspaces with unfinished files, outdated versions and failed attempts.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14364) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14364) [3](https://arebench.com) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper notes that many research-agent benchmarks emphasize standalone tasks and self-contained workflows in clean or curated workspaces, whereas real requests depend on earlier decisions and messy workspace state, so high scores do not necessarily mean reliable delivery.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14364) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14364) [3](https://arebench.com) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Tasks come from real researcher sessions, paired with in-project workspaces replayed to the moment of the request.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14364) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14364) [3](https://arebench.com) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Author background**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14364) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14364) [3](https://arebench.com) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Review process**：A three-stage pipeline: the Curator normalizes raw sessions, verifies requirements against the full interaction and labels them by a requirement taxonomy; the Builder compiles the request and still-valid history into the instruction, replays the workspace and compiles a hidden rubric into a binary checklist; the Validator calibrates the judge, checks the package, runs actors and sends every task to human review, returning failures to the Builder.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14364) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14364) [3](https://arebench.com) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Quality control**：An empty delivery should score near zero and a fabricated delivery that looks complete should score clearly below actor runs; every label cites quoted evidence, and all quotes are audited against the source.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14364) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14364) [3](https://arebench.com) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Decontamination**：The rubric and a provenance package (raw wording, source turns, path mappings, replay logs and anonymization decisions) form the hidden evaluation set.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14364) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14364) [3](https://arebench.com) (accessed 2026-11-16)
