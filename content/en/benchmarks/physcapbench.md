# PhysCapBench

A physically grounded video-captioning benchmark with 500 clips in each of three domains (third-person scenes, egocentric manipulation, multi-view handheld-gripper demonstrations), human-verified structured physical annotations, and LLM-judge scoring on seven physical dimensions. The paper reports Physis top among 11 third-person strategies at 74.07.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Evaluates whether video captions record physical information such as object motion, interactions and state evolution accurately and completely, as supervision for world-model pretraining and manipulation policies.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16424) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16424) [3](https://hfwang2001.github.io/physcapbench-site/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that existing caption evaluation rarely focuses on physical content or covers third-person scenes, egocentric human manipulation and multi-view handheld-gripper demonstrations together, and presents itself as the first unified physical-caption benchmark across the three. A separate Physis-Lang work also uses the name PhysCapBench; they are different benchmarks.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16424) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16424) [3](https://hfwang2001.github.io/physcapbench-site/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Third-person videos cover 8 primary physical categories and 16 scene categories; egocentric videos come from EgoDex, covering 118 tasks (four or five videos each) and 15 scenario groups with RGB and hand tracking, about 2.21 hours; the third domain is multi-view handheld-gripper demonstrations.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16424) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16424) [3](https://hfwang2001.github.io/physcapbench-site/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Evaluation
