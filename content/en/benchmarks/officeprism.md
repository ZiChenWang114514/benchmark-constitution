# OfficePrism

OfficePrism evaluates single-domain enterprise operations across seven business functions. OfficePrism-420 has 420 tasks (105 L1, 140 L2, 175 L3) with Chinese tickets, and code and data are on GitHub. Across 124 model–harness–package configurations, the paper reports a joint natural-language-and-database pass rate from 22.6% to 76.4%, with a median of 67.9%.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Whether agents can complete operations closed within a single business function, and how performance varies across benchmark-defined L1–L3 task structure within that function, before broader multi-function coordination.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that broad workplace scores can mix competence within one function with the extra cost of coordinating across functions and systems, making failures hard to localize. Prior enterprise benchmarks provide executable worlds or scalable construction pipelines. OfficePrism complements them by confining each task to one business function and applying the same task-structure definition within every function. This entry is distinct from the existing OfficeBench and OfficeQA records and from the biomedical benchmark PRISM, and must not be merged with them.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Each task runs in a fresh environment that combines a shared synthetic organization with exactly one of seven business functions and accesses state through typed tools. The shared organization has 75 employees (63 active) across 12 departments and 22 teams. Task counts by function: administration 56, finance 58, human resources 60, information technology 60, legal affairs 76, procurement 55, and project work 55. The agent sees caller context and a Chinese natural-language ticket.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

## Data format

**Size**：OfficePrism-420 contains 420 tasks: 105 L1, 140 L2, and 175 L3. Every function contributes 15 L1 and 20 L2 tasks; retained L3 counts range from 20 to 41. Reference witnesses span 1–4, 2–7, and 4–16 calls for L1–L3, with medians of 1, 3, and 7. Depending on the active function, the task-local environment contains 42–61 top-level collections, 4,701–17,126 records, and 135–198 native functions.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Question types**：Multi-step tool-use tasks driven by Chinese enterprise tickets; natural-language replies and database state are both scored

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Modalities**：Text tickets, structured enterprise records, and typed tool interfaces

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Languages**：The tickets shown to the agent are in Chinese

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Difficulty**：L1 expresses one direct intent; L2 adds a dependent chain whose later call consumes an earlier result; L3 combines at least two elements such as filtering, cross-entity transfer, conditional action, post-write checking, recovery, or aggregation. L1 is highest in all 124 configurations, and L2 exceeds L3 in 122.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Public and hidden split**：The paper says the same checksummed 420-task JSON is used throughout the experiments. The text states that the artifact will be released publicly upon acceptance, but the public repository https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism was already accessible on 2026-11-19.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Per-case joint natural-language-and-database pass rate; separate natural-language and database components; and the same definition on the fixed L1, L2, and L3 subsets of 105, 140, and 175 tasks.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Scoring**：The same 420 tasks are evaluated through two interface packages: full exposes native function schemas, and search uses discovery and dispatch over the same toolkit. The runner records the selected package and starts each task from an isolated state.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22262) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22262) [3](https://github.com/Leozyu/OfficePrism) (accessed 2026-11-19)
