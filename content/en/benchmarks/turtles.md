# TurtLES

A 3D turbulence surrogate benchmark: about 14 TB of implicit LES cylinder-wake data, 400 trajectories with 3 to 11 million irregular points per frame, scored on rollout accuracy, spectral fidelity and latency. The paper reports CALM-PDE with the lowest relative L2 error of four baselines.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests machine-learning surrogates on dynamic forecasting of 3D turbulence: stability of long autoregressive rollouts on dense irregular meshes, preservation of high-frequency turbulent features, and generalization across geometries and flow conditions.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09567) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09567) [3](https://github.com/itsakk/TurtLES) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/turtlesprojectml/turtles) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper notes that many so-called dynamic fluid benchmarks are unsteady laminar flows, chaotic 2D systems or 3D dynamics dominated by buoyancy, radiative forcing or magnetic effects; standard error metrics hide this, which only energy spectra, fluctuation statistics and regime transitions reveal.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09567) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09567) [3](https://github.com/itsakk/TurtLES) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/turtlesprojectml/turtles) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Rollout accuracy (relative L2 error, VRMSE), spectral fidelity (spanwise energy spectra of streamwise velocity in low, mid and high bands) and inference latency

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09567) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09567) [3](https://github.com/itsakk/TurtLES) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/turtlesprojectml/turtles) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Scoring**：Four baselines are compared under a shared training budget; because meshes are huge, training samples point subsets and inference predicts all nodes in batches.
