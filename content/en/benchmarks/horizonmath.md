# HorizonMath

A mathematical-discovery benchmark of 113 mostly unsolved applied and computational problems across eight areas, each with a verifier that checks answers by high-precision numerics and constraint checks. The paper reports GPT-5.4 Pro and GPT-5.6 Sol Pro each finding three novel solutions.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Measures whether models can make new discoveries on mostly unsolved problems in applied and computational mathematics, using the generator-verifier gap to check answers automatically.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15617) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15617) [3](https://github.com/ewang26/HorizonMath) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/squashenthus/HorizonMath) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper notes that evaluating research-level mathematics is bottlenecked by human review, and existing open-problem benchmarks are small, keep verifiers private (checking requires purchased access) or rely heavily on expert grading, limiting reproducibility.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15617) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15617) [3](https://github.com/ewang26/HorizonMath) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/squashenthus/HorizonMath) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Problem-specific automatic checks: high-precision numeric comparison where ground truth is computable (71), comparison with best-known results (33), and constraint checks on new constructions (9)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15617) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15617) [3](https://github.com/ewang26/HorizonMath) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/squashenthus/HorizonMath) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Scoring**：High-precision numeric comparison and deterministic constraint checkers, plus a compliance review of permitted operations; the paper notes that a numeric match does not formally prove a closed form exact.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15617) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15617) [3](https://github.com/ewang26/HorizonMath) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/squashenthus/HorizonMath) (accessed 2026-11-16)
