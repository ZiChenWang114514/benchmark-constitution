# HydroTuring

A physics-probe benchmark for hydrologic models: 37 probes that generate hidden cases from seeds at run time to test mass, energy and momentum conservation. Of 32 evaluated models, only the 3 exact references pass every probe among the 28 scored on at least five probes.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Treats physics as a test suite: checks whether hydrologic models, AI-based and process-based, conserve mass, energy and momentum on hidden generated cases.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper notes that AI-for-science benchmarks report accuracy on public data and, to the authors' knowledge, no benchmark in hydrology or Earth system science tests conservation laws on hidden generated cases; water-budget closure is rarely reported comparably and stays hidden for closed models.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Test cases are generated at run time from a seed, so no fixed test set exists; probes are proposed and merged by the community, with probe authors credited as co-authors.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Author background**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Review process**：Every probe must pass an acceptance gate: exact reference models must pass, otherwise the tolerance has drifted beyond what exact physics achieves and the probe is wrong; all acceptance gates were re-run on 2026-11-02.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Quality control**：Probes are validated with 77 reference models, including reference_leaky, which hides a 15% sink, and reference_in_sample, which leaks only outside its build range; over the same seeds reference_leaky fails closure 20 of 20 times.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Decontamination**：Cases are generated from seeds at run time and tolerances are hidden; an operator can add a private suite outside the repository without changing the public one.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Each criterion returns a binary result and a measured value; models are summarized by the share of probes passed

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11563) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.11563) [3](https://github.com/Flood-Lab/HydroTuring) [4](https://flood-lab.github.io/HydroTuring/) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Scoring**：Each probe contains criteria; a model that leaks water in one regime fails to conserve water even if it closes the budget elsewhere.
