# COSMIO

A cross-survey astronomical imputation benchmark: 115,000 common galaxies and 14,825 strong-lensing systems forward-rendered for LSST, Euclid and Roman, scored by CRPS and downstream lens classification. The paper reports its asynchronous diffusion baseline with the lowest average CRPS.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Impute missing survey observations across LSST, Euclid and Roman configurations from the observed modalities, and test their scientific utility through downstream strong-lens classification.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04139) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04139) [3](https://github.com/fw-ic/COSMIO) [4](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cosmidata/cosmio) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper notes that methods built for the most established missing-modality setting, multi-contrast brain MRI, transfer poorly to astronomical surveys and are often beaten by simpler methods; ground-based LSST is limited by atmospheric seeing, PSF-dominated resolution and variable noise.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04139) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04139) [3](https://github.com/fw-ic/COSMIO) [4](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cosmidata/cosmio) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS) as the primary reconstruction metric for probabilistic methods, plus lens-classification metrics for scientific utility; SSIM and LPIPS are not primary criteria

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04139) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04139) [3](https://github.com/fw-ic/COSMIO) [4](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cosmidata/cosmio) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Scoring**：Results are grouped by target survey and averaged over the corresponding imputation tasks.
