# RAVEN-Bench

A paired electro-optical and infrared aerial video question-answering benchmark with 100 temporally aligned video pairs and 1,200 independently audited four-way questions, evaluated under EO-only, IR-only and paired inputs. The paper evaluates 26 MLLMs and shows that aggregate accuracy can overstate evidence-supported reasoning: Gemini 3.1 Pro reaches 49.6% paired aggregate accuracy, which falls to 36.8% and 30.2% on consistency and coherence scores. Code is released under Apache 2.0; the dataset is gated and limited to research and non-commercial use. The paper was accepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Datasets and Benchmarks Track.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Whether models can perform target-centric grounding, temporal-spatial reasoning and modality-aware event reasoning on low-altitude paired EO–IR video, where evidence may be small, brief and present in only one stream, and whether paired input helps beyond the stronger unimodal condition, with checks for unimodal reliance, target consistency and evidence-chain coherence.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that video benchmarks mostly use natural RGB video, remote-sensing vision–language evaluations are largely image-based, and EO–IR datasets are usually framed as detection, tracking or segmentation, leaving language-grounded reasoning over paired EO–IR aerial videos largely untested.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

## Construction

**Question sources**：100 temporally aligned EO–IR video pairs with 12 questions per pair, for 1,200 human-authored four-way items. Each sample centers on one target-centric aerial event. All videos come from one aircraft, one EO/IR gimbal and one region in western North America.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Author background**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Review process**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Quality control**：Every item is independently audited for answer keys, modality requirements, evidence intervals and group validity. Related questions are placed in consistency and coherence groups so that the same target or event is revisited.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Decontamination**：Text-only and input-perturbation or frame-budget controls are reported. The best text-only setting reaches Smain 28.4, showing that visual input is needed. The release is gated, with metadata removal and exclusion of sensitive clips. The benchmark provides no annotations for detection, tracking, segmentation, geolocation or pixel-level registration.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Aggregate accuracy Smain under each modality setting, cross-modal gain Gcm (paired-input gain beyond the stronger unimodal condition), capability-level scores SL1–SL3 and gap Dcap, plus grouped consistency Scons, coherence Scoh and their difference.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Scoring**：26 MLLMs are evaluated under a constrained evidence-budget protocol, including frontier API models, open-weight models and three remote-sensing VLMs. The code repository provides evaluation scripts.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18880) [2](https://github.com/yuhu990424/RAVEN-Bench) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TerraSenseAnalytics/RAVEN-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-19)

**Saturation**：Not saturated, and aggregate accuracy overstates grouped reliability: across 26 models, Scons is 16.9 points lower than Smain on average, and Scoh is 21.8 points lower. One of the highest paired aggregate scores in Table 2 is 49.6% for Gemini 3.1 Pro.

**Contamination and disputes**：All videos come from one aircraft, one gimbal and one region, and condition subsets are too small for robustness comparisons. Paired EO–IR aerial video is dual-use; gating and terms cannot prevent every misuse after download. Some models have negative paired-input gain. UniRS does not have the highest paired aggregate accuracy but has the strongest grouped scores.
