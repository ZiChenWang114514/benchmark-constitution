# RSI-PDEbench

An identify-then-solve PDE benchmark of 1,001 classical and fractional cases over 42 templates: agents infer the hidden equation and coefficients from 256 samples and write a solver, judged by relative L2 error on hidden tests. The paper reports its harness with Opus 5.5 passing 196 of 210 Full cases.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：From sparse solution samples, an agent must identify the hidden equation family from a finite library and fit its coefficients (including fractional orders), then write a program that solves the PDE accurately over the whole domain, improving the solver from feedback within a fixed budget.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that hiding both equation family and coefficients is harder than translating a fully specified PDE into code, and that executable code alone is not evidence of scientific accuracy.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Cases are generated from classical and fractional PDE templates: 20 classical and fractional, 10 nonlinear or multiphysics, and 12 data-driven or additional-dimensional templates, with analytic or convergence-checked high-accuracy numerical references.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Review process**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Quality control**：Reference generation and thresholds are fixed independently; analytic references are sampled on the scoring grid and numerical references use refinement and convergence checks; one frozen evaluator checks all programs in a setting.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Decontamination**：Hidden test labels, reference arrays and scores are inaccessible during agent execution; public seeds generate training and validation instances while a separate secret determines hidden tests.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Pass rate: space-time and final-time relative L2 error at most 2e-3, with initial and boundary data exact to 1e-10; model calls, tokens, validation submissions and dollar cost are also recorded

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14949) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14949) [3](https://github.com/jeremylu916/RSI-PDE) [4](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JeremyLu1017/PDE-RSI) (accessed 2026-11-16)

**Scoring**：After development, an independent evaluator scores the frozen solver program on hidden cases; hidden checkpoint scores never return to the running agent.
